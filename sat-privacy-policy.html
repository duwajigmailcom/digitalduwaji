1. Who we are

SAT Math Prep (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is an app by Digital Duwaji that offers SAT math practice questions, practice tests, and explanations. This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have. “SAT” is a registered trademark of the College Board, which is not affiliated with and does not endorse this app.

Developer: Digital Duwaji. Contact: digitalduwaji@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we do not receive any data about how you use the app.

Your practice progress, scores, and settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see them, and they are not sent to us. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted.

The one exception is advertising. Google AdMob, a third-party service built into the app, collects certain information to show ads. This is explained in the next section.

3. Advertising

SAT Math Prep uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob, operated by Google, may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as device model, operating system, and app version), and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to deliver ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see an ad, and prevent fraud. Depending on your settings and where you live, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

We do not receive this data from Google. Google acts as an independent data controller for the information it collects through AdMob. To learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services, visit policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region that requires it, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app’s settings.
  • Device settings: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Google Ad Settings: you can manage personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

4. Children and students under 18

Many SAT test-takers are minors. The app is not directed to children under 13. Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, we hold no personal information about any user, including minors.

For users we know are under 18 (or under the age of consent where you live), we configure ads to be non-personalized and to follow Google’s policies for ads shown to minors. Users aged 13 to 17 should review this policy with a parent or guardian.

5. How we share information

We do not have personal information to sell or share. The only data sharing that happens through the app is between your device and Google AdMob, as described in the Advertising section. We may disclose information if required by law, but we hold no user data that could be disclosed.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep no user data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Information stored on your device (such as your progress) is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. To remove advertising data held by Google, use the controls in Google Ad Settings or your device’s advertising ID settings described above.

7. Security

Your practice data stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security features. Data sent to AdMob is transmitted using encryption as provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use the app without giving us any personal information. You can change your ad personalization choices at any time as described above. Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, UK, or California), you may have rights over personal information held by Google through its advertising services. You can exercise those rights with Google at myaccount.google.com. For questions about this app, contact us below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we add new features that involve collecting data, such as accounts or analytics, we will update this policy before they take effect. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version.
